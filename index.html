About VIZFARM

A farmer-owned company built for what comes next.

Who We Are

VIZFARM Farmer Producer Company Limited is a farmer-focused organization established to strengthen agricultural livelihoods through collective action, business development, value addition, and improved market access.

Our Vision

To build a sustainable and modern agricultural ecosystem where farmers have better access to markets, technology, business opportunities, and value-added agriculture.

Our Mission

  • Strengthen farmer livelihoods
  • Improve market access
  • Promote sustainable agriculture
  • Develop value-added agricultural products
  • Explore national and international markets
  • Connect agriculture with modern logistics and infrastructure
  • Encourage innovation and technology in farming

What We Do
